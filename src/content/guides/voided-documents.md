# Voided documents

Every cancelled document is in one place: **Accounting > Voided documents**. Invoices, credit notes, cash sales, payments received, bills, supplier payments, expenses, other income and bank transfers are all there.

Each row shows:

- the document, its date and who it was for;
- **why** it was voided (the reason typed when it was voided);
- **who** voided it and when;
- what happened in the books.

Filter by kind and by dates. From a month's page, **See which** opens the documents voided in that month.

## What happens in the books

- **Removed**: the document's month was still open, so its entries were taken out of the books.
- **Reversed**: the month was already closed, so a reversing entry was posted in the open month. The closed month did not change.

## A void is never undone

A voided document is not brought back. Its number stays used, so your numbering has no gaps that auditors and KRA would ask about. The audit trail keeps showing that it was cancelled, and closed months stay closed.

## Copy it as a new draft

Voided an invoice or bill by mistake, or need it again with a change? Open it, or find it in the register, and click **Copy as a new draft**. The draft has the same customer or supplier, lines, prices, tax and terms. Change what you need, then issue it as usual. It gets a new number.

The two documents are linked both ways. The new one shows *Replaces INV-0006*, and the voided one shows *Replaced by INV-0009*. Each document can be copied once; after that, the button opens its replacement.

Other kinds, such as payments and expenses, are entered again from their own pages.
