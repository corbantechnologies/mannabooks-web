# Price lists

A **price list** gives a group of customers their own prices: wholesale buyers, staff, or a contract customer.

## Making a price list

1. Open **Sales > Price lists** and choose **New price list**.
2. Set what happens to products you don't list: for example **-10** sells them 10% below the normal price, **0** keeps the normal price.
3. Add products with their own fixed price on this list.
4. Open each customer and choose the price list under **Price list**.

## How it is used

When you pick a product on a quotation, invoice, cash sale or at the till, the price comes from that customer's price list. You can still change the price on any line.

A price list used by customers can't be deleted; move them first or mark it inactive.
