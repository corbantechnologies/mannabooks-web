# Manufacturing

For businesses that make what they sell: blocks, furniture, bakery goods, animal feed.

## Recipes

A **recipe** (bill of materials) lists the components that make a product, for example *10 concrete blocks = 1 bag of cement + 3 bags of sand*. The product and its components must all track stock.

## Production

1. **Inventory > Production > New production order**: choose the recipe and how many to make. The components needed are worked out.
2. When it is made, open the order, change any component quantity that was really different, and choose **Complete**.

The components come out of stock at their average cost, and the finished products go in at that total cost divided by the quantity made. Their cost then flows to cost of sales when they are sold.
