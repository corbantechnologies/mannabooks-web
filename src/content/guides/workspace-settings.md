# Workspace settings

**Settings** holds the details that appear on your documents and KRA submissions. Owners and Admins can change them; everyone else can see the workspace name.

## Business details

| Field | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | The name people see in MannaBooks and on documents |
| Registered (legal) name | The name on your certificate of incorporation, if different |
| KRA PIN | Required for VAT and eTIMS. Format: one letter, nine digits, one letter (P051234567X) |
| Registered for VAT | Tick if you charge VAT. You must enter your KRA PIN first |
| Currency | The currency your books are kept in, usually KES |
| Financial year starts in | The first month of your financial year, used by reports and year-end |

## Numbering

Each kind of record has its own number series, for example employees **EMP-0001**. A series appears the first time it is used. You can change its prefix, or move the next number **up** (never down), so numbers are never reused.

## Deleting a workspace

Only an **Owner** can delete a workspace, and must type its name to confirm. Nobody can open it afterwards; its records are kept for audit.
