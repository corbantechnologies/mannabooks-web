# Purchase orders (LPOs)

A **purchase order** (Local Purchase Order) tells a supplier what you want to buy and at what price.

## Raising an order

1. Open **Purchasing > Purchase orders** and choose **New order**.
2. Choose the supplier, the expected delivery date and the warehouse it goes to.
3. Add the products, quantities and agreed costs.
4. **Save**. It stays a draft until someone allowed to approve orders chooses **Approve**.
5. **Print LPO** and send it to the supplier.

Only people whose role has *Approve purchase and service orders* can approve. Store managers can raise orders but not approve them.

## What happens next

- Goods arrive: **Receive goods** on the order (see *Goods received*). The order shows how much of each line has arrived: *Partly received*, then *Received*.
- The rest isn't coming: **Close** the order.
- Ordered by mistake: **Cancel** it, as long as nothing has been received.
