# Expenses

Use **Spend money** for costs paid there and then: fuel, airtime, rent, repairs, bank charges, petty cash spending.

1. Choose how you paid and the account the money came from.
2. Enter who you paid, or choose a supplier.
3. Add a line for each cost with its **account** (for example *Transport and fuel*) and **VAT**. Amounts include VAT by default, as on most receipts.
4. Enter the **eTIMS CU number** from the receipt: KRA only allows the VAT to be claimed with it.
5. **Save expense**. It is posted at once.

For a supplier's invoice you will pay later, enter a **bill** instead, so it shows in what you owe.

An expense entered by mistake can be **voided**, unless it is in a finished bank reconciliation.
