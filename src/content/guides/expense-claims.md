# Expense claims

Spent your own money on the business, such as fuel on a field visit, matatu fares or a client lunch? Claim it back under **People > Expense claims**.

## Making a claim

1. **New claim**, and say what it was for (for example *Field visit to Nakuru*).
2. Add each thing you paid for: the date, what it was, the amount, and **a photo or file of the receipt**. On a phone, the file box takes a photo.
3. **Send for approval**. Every line needs its receipt first.

## Approving (finance)

Claims waiting are under **To approve**. **Approve** shows every line:

- untick anything that isn't a business expense and **say why**: the person sees your reason, and only the rest is approved;
- choose the account each line goes to (fuel to *Transport and fuel*, and so on). Left as it is, it goes to *Staff expenses*.

If an approval rule covers *Staff expense claims* (Organisation > Approval rules), the claim goes through that chain first, for example the person's supervisor and then finance. Nobody approves their own claim.

Approving books the expenses and what is owed to the person (*Staff expense claims payable*).

## Paying back

Under **To pay back**, **Pay back**:

- **Now, from an account**: a bank, M-Pesa or cash account, on a date; or
- **On their next payslip**: added as a refund. It raises their take-home pay but isn't taxed and doesn't count for NSSF, SHIF or the Housing Levy. When that payroll is approved, the claim shows as paid back.
