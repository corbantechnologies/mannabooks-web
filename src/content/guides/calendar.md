# Calendar

**Work > Calendar** puts everything with a date on one timeline, so nobody has to ask around in DMs who is away or what is due.

## What it shows

- **Events** put on by hand: a board meeting, a launch, a training day. For everyone, or for one department.
- **Task deadlines** and **work reports due**: the ones you can see.
- **Leave**: everyone's approved leave if your role can see leave; otherwise your own department's.
- **Pay and tax**: payday (for payroll staff), and for finance the 9th (PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, Housing Levy) and the 20th (VAT, if you're registered).
- **Public holidays**, from Settings.

Tick the colours at the top to hide or show each kind. Click anything to see more, and **Open** to go to it.

## Adding an event

**Add event**, or the **+** on a day. Choose who sees it. Anyone can add an event for their own department; an event for everyone, or for another department, needs the *Company calendar: manage* permission (owners and admins have it). Whoever added an event can remove it.
