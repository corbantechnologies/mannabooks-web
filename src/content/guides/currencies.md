# Currencies and exchange rates

Your books are kept in one **base currency**, set when the workspace was created (usually KES). It can't be changed or switched off.

## Other currencies

USD is switched on, and EUR, GBP, UGX, TZS and RWF are ready to switch on. **Add currency** for any other. Switch off currencies you don't use so they don't clutter lists.

## Exchange rates

A rate says how much one unit of a currency is worth in the base currency on a date, for example 1 USD = 129.50 KES. **Record a rate** whenever you need one, typically the Central Bank of Kenya rate for the day.

When a document in a foreign currency is posted, MannaBooks uses the latest rate on or before its date. If there is none, it asks you to record one first.
