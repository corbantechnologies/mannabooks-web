# Goods received (GRN)

A **goods received note** records a delivery from a supplier.

## Receiving against an order

1. Open the approved purchase order and choose **Receive goods**.
2. Enter what actually arrived on each line. You can receive part of an order now and the rest later.
3. Enter the supplier's delivery note number and **Save delivery**.

Stock items go straight into the warehouse at the order's cost before VAT. Their value is held in **Goods received not invoiced** until the supplier's bill arrives.

## Receiving without an order

Choose **Receive goods** in **Purchasing > Goods received**, choose the supplier and add the products, quantities and costs.

## Then

Choose **Enter their bill** on the delivery when the invoice comes. A delivery entered by mistake can be **voided** before its bill is entered, as long as the stock is still there.
