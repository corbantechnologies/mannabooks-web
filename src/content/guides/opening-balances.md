# Opening balances

**Opening balances** are what each account held the day before you started using MannaBooks: cash, bank and M-Pesa, stock, what customers owe you, what you owe suppliers and KRA, loans and capital.

## Entering them

1. Choose the **as at** date: usually the last day before your first day on MannaBooks. It must fall in an open month; if it is before your first financial year, add an earlier year first.
2. Enter each balance as a debit (assets) or a credit (liabilities and equity). Take the figures from your last balance sheet or trial balance, bank statements and M-Pesa statements.
3. **Save draft** as you go, then **Post opening balances**.

If debits and credits differ, the difference goes to **Opening balance equity**, so you can post even if you don't have every figure yet. Clear it later with a journal entry once you know where it belongs.

## Changing them

Choose **Unpost to edit**, change the figures and post again. This is only possible while the month is open.

Income and expense accounts are not listed: they start at zero in a new year.
