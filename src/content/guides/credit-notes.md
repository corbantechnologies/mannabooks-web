# Credit notes

A **credit note** reduces what a customer owes: goods returned, an overcharge, or a discount agreed after the sale. KRA requires one; you can't just edit an issued invoice.

## Crediting an invoice

1. Open the invoice and choose **Credit note**, or open **Sales > Credit notes** and choose **New credit note**.
2. Enter the date and the reason.
3. Choose **Credit the whole invoice**, or **Credit part of it** and enter just the lines and quantities being credited.
4. **Issue credit note**.

It is applied to the invoice straight away. If it is more than the invoice still owes, the rest stays as the customer's credit, which their next payment or invoice can use.

A credit note without an invoice is kept entirely as the customer's credit.

## Voiding

A credit note raised in error can be voided with a reason, unless KRA eTIMS has signed it.
