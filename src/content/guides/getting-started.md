# Getting started

MannaBooks runs your business in one place: sales, purchasing, stock, accounting, payroll and KRA compliance. Everything happens inside a **workspace**, which is one business.

## 1. Create your account and workspace

Sign up with your name, email, a password and your **business name**. MannaBooks creates your account and your business **workspace** together, and you become its **Owner**. It sets up:

- the preset roles (Owner, Admin, Accountant, Sales, Cashier, Store manager, HR manager, Self-service);
- a default branch called *Head office*;
- a chart of accounts, tax rates, currencies and your current financial year.

One account works for every business you belong to. To add another business later, use **New workspace** on **Your workspaces**.

If a colleague **invited** you, open their invitation link and sign up from there: you join their workspace and no new one is created.

## 2. Choose what you want to do first

The first time an Owner or Admin opens the **Overview**, MannaBooks asks what the business wants to do first:

| Goal | Switches on |
| --- | --- |
| Send invoices and get paid | Sales and invoicing, Cash and banking |
| Run a shop with a till | Sales and invoicing, Point of sale, Purchasing, Stock and warehouses, Cash and banking |
| Manage stock and warehouses | Purchasing, Stock and warehouses |
| Run jobs and projects | Sales and invoicing, Purchasing, Cash and banking, Projects |
| Make products | Sales and invoicing, Purchasing, Stock and warehouses, Manufacturing, Cash and banking |
| Run payroll | Leave and payroll, Cash and banking |
| Everything | Every module |

The menu then shows only those modules, so it stays short. Accounting, reports, customers, products, suppliers, employees and settings are always there. You can switch any module on or off later in **Settings > Modules**; switching one off only hides it, and nothing is deleted.

## 3. Follow the checklist

The **Overview** then shows a short checklist for your goal, for example *Add your KRA PIN*, *Add your products with the stock you have* and *Open a till shift and make a sale*. Each step ticks itself off when it is done, and the list disappears once everything is set up. You can also **Hide this list** sooner; bring it back in **Settings**.

## Your Overview

The **Overview** is yours to arrange. Choose **Customise** to add, remove and reorder what you see: **Coming up**, this period's figures, **Cash and bank** (what's in each account), **Money in and out**, **Tasks**, the **Business inbox** and your role. It changes only your Overview, not anyone else's, and shows only what your role can see.

**Coming up** (also in the menu) lists everything with a due date, soonest first: suppliers' bills, approved payment vouchers, salaries, PAYE, SHIF, NSSF and the Housing Levy (due by the 9th), VAT (by the 20th, if you're registered), and what customers owe you. It's grouped into overdue, the next 7 days and later, with totals to pay and to collect. Choose how far ahead to look, from 7 to 90 days, and open any line to pay or chase it.

## Getting around

- The left menu shows only the pages your role allows.
- **Find a page quickly:** press **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on a Mac), or tap the search button at the top. Type part of a page's name, such as *tasks* or *invoices*, then press **Enter**.
- **Menu sections:** only the section of the page you're on is open. Tap another heading (Sales, People...) to look inside; it folds again when you move to another page.
- **Pin pages you use most:** hover over a menu item (on a phone, tap the star beside it) and choose the star. Pinned pages appear at the top of the menu, and first in the quick search. Use the arrows beside a pinned page to put them in your order.
- **Move a whole section up:** the star beside a section's name (Work, People, Organisation...) moves it to the top of the menu, under Overview. Your pins follow you to every phone and computer you sign in on.
- The **Guide** button on each page opens the guide for that page.
- **Switch workspace** at the top takes you back to your list of businesses.
- Under your name (top right): **My account** to change your password or your sign-in email (a link goes to the new address, and the email changes only once you open it), and **Sign out**.
