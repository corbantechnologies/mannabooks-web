# Supplier payments

Every payment to a supplier is listed in **Purchasing > Supplier payments**. Most are made from payment vouchers; you can also record one directly with **Record payment**.

## Withholding tax

If you keep back withholding tax, enter it. The bill is settled by what you paid **plus** what you withheld. The withheld amount goes to **Withholding tax payable**, which you pay to KRA and issue the supplier a certificate for.

## Deposits and paying in advance

Sometimes you pay before the supplier's bill exists: a deposit for a job, or paying up front. Record it with **Record payment** and don't apply it to any bill. It shows as **Not applied** on the payment, and the supplier's balance shows they owe you that much in work or goods.

When the bill comes:

1. Enter the bill for the **full** amount and **Post** it.
2. On the bill, a note shows the money already paid to this supplier in advance. Choose **Apply**, and only the rest is left to pay. Or open the payment and choose **Apply to bills** to split it across several bills (**Fill oldest first** does it for you).
3. Pay the rest with a payment voucher, as usual.

A deposit can only go on a posted bill, and never more than what's left on it.

**Withholding tax and deposits.** Tax is withheld when you pay, so withhold on the deposit too if the rules apply to it. If you agreed what the supplier takes home, gross the fee up: the fee is what they take home divided by 0.95 for 5% (100,000 ÷ 0.95 = 105,263). Enter the bill at that fee and withhold 5% of it.

## Mistakes

**Void** a payment entered by mistake, or a bounced cheque. Its bills become unpaid again and its voucher goes back to *Approved*.
