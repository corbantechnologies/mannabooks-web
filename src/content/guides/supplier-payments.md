# Supplier payments

Every payment to a supplier is listed in **Purchasing > Supplier payments**. Most are made from payment vouchers; you can also record one directly with **Record payment**.

## Withholding tax

If you keep back withholding tax, enter it. The bill is settled by what you paid **plus** what you withheld. The withheld amount goes to **Withholding tax payable**, which you pay to KRA and issue the supplier a certificate for.

## Mistakes

**Void** a payment entered by mistake, or a bounced cheque. Its bills become unpaid again and its voucher goes back to *Approved*.
