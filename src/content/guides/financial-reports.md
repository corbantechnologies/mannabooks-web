# Financial reports

Open **Accounting > Reports**. Every report can be printed or saved as a CSV file for Excel.

## Profit and loss

Income less costs for a period. Pick a period (this month, last quarter, this year...) or your own dates, and compare with the previous period or the same period last year. Filter by **cost centre**, **project** or **branch** to see the profit of one part of the business.

The report is laid out as: sales, less **cost of sales**, gives **gross profit**; less operating expenses gives **operating profit**; plus other income and less other expenses gives **net profit**.

## Balance sheet

What the business owns (assets) and owes (liabilities) on a date, and the owners' share (equity). The year's profit appears in equity until the year is closed. Total assets always equal liabilities plus equity; a badge confirms it.

## Aged receivables and payables

Who owes you, and whom you owe, grouped by how late the invoices or bills are: not yet due, 1 to 30 days, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and over 90 days. Click a name to see the documents. Use it every week to chase payments and plan what to pay.

## Sales and stock by category

Each product category's sales for a period (before VAT, less credit notes), its share of all sales, and the value of the stock in it now. Use it to see which lines make your money and where your cash is tied up in stock.

## VAT summary

Sales and purchases by VAT rate for a period, with output VAT, input VAT and the VAT to pay. These are the figures for your VAT return on iTax, due by the 20th of the following month. Only claim input VAT on purchases with the supplier's eTIMS invoice number.
