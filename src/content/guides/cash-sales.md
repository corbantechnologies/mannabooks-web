# Cash sales

A **cash sale** is a sale paid on the spot by cash, M-Pesa or card. There is no invoice to chase: the sale and the money are recorded together.

## Making a cash sale

1. Open **Sales > Cash sales** and choose **New cash sale**.
2. Choose a customer, or leave it as a walk-in. A walk-in buyer who wants their KRA PIN on the receipt can give it.
3. Choose how they paid and enter the M-Pesa code or reference. The money goes to the matching account (Cash, M-Pesa or Bank); you can change it.
4. Add the lines. Prices usually include VAT on a cash sale, so **Prices include VAT** starts ticked.
5. **Save cash sale**, then **Print** the receipt.

**Discount on the whole document.** Under the lines, enter a percentage or an amount off the whole document, for example 10% or KES 500. On saving, MannaBooks spreads it over the lines as line discounts, in proportion to each line's value, because KRA eTIMS records discounts line by line. Each line then shows its share, and the box starts empty again. A fixed amount comes off the total including VAT.

**Paid with a gift voucher.** Enter its code under **Gift voucher** and choose **Check**. It pays as much as it holds; the rest is paid by the method you chose. See [Gift vouchers](gift-vouchers).

Saving numbers it (RCT-00001), posts the sale, the VAT and the money received, and sends it to KRA eTIMS when eTIMS is switched on.

## Mistakes

A cash sale keyed by mistake can be **voided** with a reason. For goods brought back later, use a credit note.
