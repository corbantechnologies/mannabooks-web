# Invoices

An **invoice** asks a customer to pay for goods or services you supplied on credit.

## Draft, then issue

1. Open **Sales > Invoices** and choose **New invoice**.
2. Choose the customer. The due date follows their payment terms; you can change it.
3. Add the lines. Tick **Prices include VAT** if the prices you type already include VAT.
4. **Save draft** to keep working on it, or **Save and issue**.

A draft can be changed or deleted freely and is not in the books. **Issuing** gives it its number (INV-00001), records what the customer owes, the sale and the VAT you owe KRA, and sends it to KRA eTIMS when eTIMS is switched on.

## After issuing

An issued invoice can't be edited: the customer and KRA already have it. To correct it:

- **Wrong amount or goods returned:** issue a **credit note** against it.
- **Raised by mistake and not paid:** **Void** it with a reason. Voiding removes it from the books. An invoice with payments, or one signed by KRA, can't be voided; use a credit note.

## Getting paid

Choose **Record payment** on the invoice. The status moves from **Issued** to **Partially paid** to **Paid**, and invoices past their due date show as **Overdue**.

## More you can do from an invoice

- **M-Pesa prompt**: ask the customer's phone for the payment (needs M-Pesa connected).
- **More > Share link with customer**, **Email a payment reminder**, **Delivery note** and **Debit note**.
- **More > Send by email**: the customer gets the lines, the total, how to pay, a link to pay online and, where PDFs are set up, the invoice as a **PDF**.
- **More > Download PDF** or **Print or save as PDF**: an A4 copy with your logo, KRA PIN and the eTIMS details once signed.
