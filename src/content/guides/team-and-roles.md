# Team, roles and access

People sign in to a workspace through a **membership**, and each membership has a **role**. A role is a list of permissions, written as *module: action* (for example *Employees: manage*).

## Preset roles

| Role | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Full access, including deleting the workspace. There is always at least one |
| Admin | Full access to every module |
| Accountant | Finance, banking and reports |
| Sales | Customers, quotations, invoices and payments |
| Cashier | Point of sale and cash receipts |
| Store manager | Stock, warehouses and purchasing |
| HR manager | Employees, departments, leave and payroll |
| Self-service | An employee's own record, payslips and leave |

Preset roles can't be deleted, but you can change what they allow (except the Owner, which always has full access). For anything else, create a **custom role** and tick its permissions. Permissions for new modules appear as those modules are released.

## Inviting someone

On **Team and access**, choose **Invite**, enter their email and pick a role. They get a link that works for **7 days**. You can copy the link or revoke the invitation while it is pending. To give an existing employee a login, use **Invite to sign in** on their employee record instead.

## Changing access

- Change someone's **role** from the list; it applies immediately.
- **Suspend** stops someone signing in to this workspace without removing them; **Reactivate** restores access.
- **Remove** takes them out of the workspace.
- Only an Owner can make someone an Owner, and the last Owner can't be removed or demoted.

## Limiting someone to a department

In **Organisation > Team and access**, the **Sees** column sets what each person can see: the whole business, or only one department. Someone limited to a department:

- sees only documents tagged with that department or one of its cost centres, plus anything they created themselves;
- sees that department's profit and loss, and its column in profit and loss by department;
- doesn't see reports on the whole business: the balance sheet, trial balance, journals, account ledgers, VAT summary, budgets and the business summary on the dashboard.

Their role still decides what they can do; the limit decides which records. Owners always see the whole business, and nobody can change their own limit. Customers, suppliers and products are shared by every department.
