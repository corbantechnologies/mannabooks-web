# Help and feedback

Reach the MannaBooks team from inside the app: questions, problems, billing, complaints and ideas.

1. Open **Help > Help and feedback** and choose **New message**.
2. Choose what it's about, give it a subject and tell us what happened: what you did, what you saw and what you expected. Numbers and document references help.
3. We reply in the same conversation and by email. Answer there to keep everything together.
4. When it's sorted, choose **It's sorted**.

Help and feedback works even when a business is read-only or blocked, so you can always reach us.

Each person sees the messages they sent. Most questions are also answered in the user guides.
