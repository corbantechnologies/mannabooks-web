# Customer payments

Record money a customer pays you against their invoices.

## Recording a payment

1. Open **Sales > Payments** and choose **Record payment**, or choose **Record payment** on an invoice.
2. Choose the customer and enter the amount and date.
3. Choose how they paid. For **M-Pesa**, enter the transaction code (for example SJK3L2M9XX): it is required, and it lets you match the payment to your M-Pesa statement.
4. Choose the account the money went into: Cash, M-Pesa or a bank account.
5. Apply it to their unpaid invoices. **Apply oldest first** fills them from the oldest due date. Anything not applied stays as the customer's **credit**.

Saving posts the payment: the money goes into the account you chose and the customer owes less.

## Mistakes and bounced cheques

Open the payment and choose **Void** with a reason. The invoices it paid become unpaid again and the money comes out of the account.
