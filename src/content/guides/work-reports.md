# Work reports

Managers and supervisors can ask the people below them for a report (weekly sales and visits, a monthly site report, a daily cash summary) once or on a schedule. These are work reports, separate from the financial reports.

## Asking for a report

1. Open **People > Work reports** and choose **Ask for a report**.
2. Give it a title and say **what to include**. You can attach a **template** (a Word or Excel form) for people to fill in.
3. Choose **who**: everyone who reports to you (set by *Reports to* on employee records, at any depth), a department you head, or particular people.
4. Choose **how often** (once, every working day, every week, every month) and the **first due date**, and how many days before to remind them.

You can ask only people below you in the reporting line. HR managers and owners can ask anyone.

## What happens next

- Everyone asked gets an email, and the report appears under **My reports**.
- They get a reminder before it is due.
- When the due date passes, reports not in are marked **Overdue** and you get an email. For recurring requests, the next round opens.
- When a report comes in, you get an email. Open it under **To review**, then **Accept** it or **Send back** with what needs to change.

**Close** a request to stop further rounds.

## Submitting your report

Open **Work reports > My reports**, choose the report, and write it, attach a file (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or a photo, up to 10 MB), or both. Then **Submit report**. It works on a phone too. If it is sent back, the reason is shown at the top; change it and submit again.
