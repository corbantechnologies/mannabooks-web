# Gift vouchers

A gift voucher is paid for now and spent later, by the buyer or by someone they give it to. Until it is spent, its value belongs to the holder, so MannaBooks records it as money the business **owes**, not as a sale.

## Selling a voucher

1. Open **Sales > Gift vouchers** and choose **Sell a voucher**.
2. Enter its **value**, and a **valid until** date if it should expire. Leave the date blank for a voucher that never expires.
3. Choose how it was paid for (cash, M-Pesa, bank or card) and the account the money went into.
4. Optionally add who it is **for** (printed on the voucher) and the customer who bought it.
5. Leave **Code** blank and MannaBooks makes a code that is hard to guess, such as `K7QM-3XRP`.

Then open the voucher and choose **Voucher PDF** to print it or send it to the person it is for. It shows the code, its value, the expiry date and a QR code of the code.

**In the books:** the money goes into cash, M-Pesa or the bank, and **Gift vouchers outstanding** (a liability) goes up by the same amount. No VAT is charged when a voucher is sold. VAT is charged on the goods when the voucher is spent, like any other sale.

## Giving a voucher away

Someone who manages gift vouchers (admins and accountants) can choose **Given free** under **How**, for a promotion or to make up for a bad experience. Its value is recorded as **Promotional vouchers**, a cost, and is owed to the holder in the same way.

## Spending a voucher

- **At the till:** tap **Discount or gift voucher**, enter the code and choose **Check**. The voucher pays as much of the sale as it holds; take the rest by cash, M-Pesa or card. If it covers everything, choose **Paid by the voucher: finish sale**.
- **On a cash sale:** enter the code under **Gift voucher**. An M-Pesa code is only needed for what the voucher doesn't cover.
- **Against an invoice:** record a payment with the method **Gift voucher** and type the code in **Voucher code**. No money is received; the amount comes off the voucher.

A voucher can be spent in parts until nothing is left on it. Codes can be typed in small letters and without the dash.

**In the books:** the sale is recorded in full, with its VAT, and the part the voucher paid reduces **Gift vouchers outstanding** instead of adding cash.

## Mistakes

- **A sale or payment is voided:** what the voucher paid goes back onto it.
- **A voucher was sold by mistake:** open it and choose **Void**, then refund the buyer separately. Only a voucher nothing has been spent from can be voided.

## When a voucher expires

Every night MannaBooks expires vouchers whose date has passed. What was left on them moves from **Gift vouchers outstanding** to **Unspent vouchers**, which is income. An expired voucher can't be spent.

The list on **Gift vouchers** shows **Still to be spent**: the total value of the active vouchers. It should match the balance of **Gift vouchers outstanding** in the trial balance.

## Who can do what

- **View** vouchers and their history: sales staff, cashiers, accountants, admins.
- **Sell** vouchers: sales staff, cashiers, accountants, admins.
- **Give vouchers free and void them:** accountants and admins.
- **Check and spend** a voucher: anyone who can make a sale or record a payment.
