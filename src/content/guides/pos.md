# Point of sale

The **till** is a fast screen for selling over the counter. Each sale is a cash sale linked to the cashier's **shift**.

## Before you start

A manager adds the **tills** (**Point of sale > Tills**): a name, the branch, and which accounts cash, M-Pesa and card money go into.

## Selling

1. Open **Point of sale > Till** and open a shift: choose the till and count the cash already in the drawer (the float).
2. Tap products, search, or scan a barcode (the product code) and press Enter.
   For a product with other units (a carton, a crate), change the unit under its name in the cart; the price changes with it.
3. For a loyalty customer, enter their phone or card number: their prices and points appear.
4. Choose how they pay:
   - **Cash**: enter the cash received to see the change.
   - **M-Pesa**: send a prompt to their phone, or type the code from their M-Pesa message.
   - **Card**: optionally enter the slip number.
5. **Print slip** for an 80 mm receipt printer, or go straight to the next sale.

## Closing the shift

Choose **Close shift** and count the drawer. MannaBooks compares it with what it expected (float plus cash sales). A difference is posted to **Cash over and short**, so the books match the drawer. **Shifts** shows every shift with its takings by payment method.

Each cashier sees their own shifts; managers see all of them.
