# Customers

A **customer** is anyone you sell to on credit or want on a receipt by name. Each one gets a number (CUS-0001) and a running balance.

## Adding a customer

1. Open **Sales > Customers** and choose **New customer**.
2. Enter their name, phone and email. Add their **KRA PIN** if they are a business: KRA needs it on the invoice for them to claim the VAT.
3. Set **payment terms** in days (for example 30). New invoices for them get a due date that many days after the invoice date.

## Balance and statement

The **balance** is what they owe: invoices less payments and credit notes. A negative balance means they have paid ahead and are in credit.

Open a customer to see their **statement**: every invoice, payment and credit note in date order with a running balance. Print it and send it when you chase payment.

A customer with documents can't be deleted. Mark them **inactive** instead to hide them from lists.
