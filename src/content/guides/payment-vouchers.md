# Payment vouchers

A **payment voucher** is a request to pay a supplier, approved before the money goes out.

1. Open **Purchasing > Payment vouchers** and choose **New voucher**, or **Pay** on a bill or supplier.
2. Choose the bills and how much of each to pay.
3. **Withholding tax** is worked out from the supplier's rate on the amount before VAT. You can change it.
4. **Send for approval**.
5. Someone with approval rights, and not the person who prepared it, chooses **Approve**. (The Owner may approve their own.)
6. Whoever makes the payment chooses **Record payment**: the date, method, the account it came from and the cheque or transfer reference.

**Print** the voucher for signatures and your files. A paid voucher shows the payment it created.
