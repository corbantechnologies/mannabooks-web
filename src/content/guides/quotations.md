# Quotations

A **quotation** is a price you offer before the customer commits. It does not touch the books.

## Making a quotation

1. Open **Sales > Quotations** and choose **New quotation**.
2. Choose the customer, the date and how long the offer is valid.
3. Add the lines: pick a product or type a description, then quantity, price, discount and VAT.
4. Save it, then **Print** or save it as PDF to send to the customer.

## Following it up

Mark the quotation **Sent**, then **Accepted** or **Declined** when you hear back. Quotations past their valid-until date show as expired.

When the customer accepts, choose **Convert to invoice**. MannaBooks creates a draft invoice with the same lines; check it and issue it.
