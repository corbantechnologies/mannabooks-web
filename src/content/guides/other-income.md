# Other income

Use **Receive money** for money that isn't a sale to a customer: bank interest, rent you receive, commission, a grant, a refund from a supplier.

Choose the account it went into, who it came from, and the **income account** for each line (blank uses *Other income*). If VAT was charged (for example on commercial rent), choose the rate.

Payments from customers for invoices belong in **Sales > Payments**, and sales paid on the spot in **Cash sales**.
