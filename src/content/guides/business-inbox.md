# Business inbox

When your customer or supplier also uses MannaBooks, you can send them documents straight into their books instead of by email. They don't retype anything: one click turns your invoice into their bill.

## How MannaBooks finds them

By **KRA PIN**. If the KRA PIN on your customer or supplier matches the KRA PIN of a business on MannaBooks, a button **Send to *their name* on MannaBooks** appears on:

- an issued **invoice**, which arrives as an invoice;
- a sent or accepted **quotation**;
- an approved **purchase order (LPO)**.

Check the KRA PIN on the customer or supplier if the button doesn't appear. If they aren't on MannaBooks, send the document by email as usual.

## Sending

1. Open the document and choose **Send to … on MannaBooks**.
2. Add a message if you like, and **Send**.
3. **Business inbox > Sent** shows each document you sent and what they did with it: *New*, *Accepted*, or *Declined* with their reason. A declined document can be fixed and sent again.

## Receiving

Documents sent to you appear in **Business inbox**, with a count in the menu. Open one to see its lines and totals, then:

| You receive | Accepting creates |
| --- | --- |
| A supplier's invoice | A **draft bill**, with the supplier (found by KRA PIN or added for you), their invoice number, dates and lines. Choose the account to record it in. |
| A supplier's quotation | A **draft purchase order** to that supplier with the quoted lines |
| A customer's purchase order (LPO) | A **draft invoice** to that customer, with the LPO number as the reference |

Drafts are never posted for you: check them, then post the bill, approve the order or issue the invoice as usual.

If your business isn't registered for VAT, the VAT on a supplier's invoice becomes part of the cost on your bill.

**Decline** a document you don't agree with and say why; the sender sees your reason.

## Who can do what

Owners and Admins can do everything. **Accountants** can receive and accept; **Sales** and **Store managers** can send. Accepting also needs the right to create the draft (bills, purchase orders or invoices).
