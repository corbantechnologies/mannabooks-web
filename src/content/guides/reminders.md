# Payment reminders

MannaBooks can email customers about unpaid invoices: before the due date, on it, and after it.

## Switching them on

1. Open **Sales > Payment reminders**.
2. Four reminders are ready: 3 days before due, on the due date, 7 days overdue and 30 days overdue. They start **off**.
3. Tick the ones you want. Click a reminder to change its timing, subject or message.

In messages you can use **{customer}**, **{number}**, **{balance}**, **{currency}**, **{due_date}** and **{business}**.

## How they are sent

Every morning at 8:00, each reminder that is on goes to customers whose invoices match it. Each reminder is sent at most once per invoice. Customers need an email address; replies go to your workspace email.

You can also send one at any time from an invoice: **More > Email a payment reminder**. Everything sent is listed under **Sent**.
