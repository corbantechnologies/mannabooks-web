# My leave and pay

Every member of staff with a MannaBooks sign-in has **My leave and pay**. It works well on a phone.

- See how many **annual leave days** you have left this year.
- **Ask for leave** and follow your requests. You can cancel a request before it starts.
- Open your **payslips** once each month's payroll is approved, and save or print them as PDF.

You only ever see your own information. If the page says your sign-in isn't linked, ask HR to link your employee record to your sign-in.
