# Salary advances

A salary advance is pay someone has already earned, or will earn this month, paid early. It is taken back on their next payslips automatically, so nobody has to remember to deduct it.

## Asking for an advance

Staff ask from **My leave and pay**, or from **People > Salary advances**, on their phone or computer. They see how much they can ask for before they send it, and can add a reason.

- **Monthly salaried staff** can ask for up to a share of their usual net pay (50% unless the business changes it). They choose how many payslips to pay it back over, up to the business's limit (3 months unless changed).
- **Daily and hourly staff** can only draw pay they have **already earned** since they were last paid. Finance confirms the days or hours worked when approving it, and it all comes off their next pay.
- Someone **paid at the end of each day** has no earned pay waiting, so there's nothing to draw.
- One advance at a time: a new one can be asked for once the last one is paid back.

Finance can also ask on someone's behalf, for staff without a login.

## Approving and paying

1. **Their supervisor or department head recommends it.** This is the person in *Reports to* on their employee record, or their department head. If they have neither (or neither can sign in), the request goes straight to finance.
2. **Finance approves it.** Anyone allowed to approve advances (the Accountant role, Admins and the Owner) can approve the amount asked for or less, and set how many payslips it comes back over. For waged staff they enter the days or hours worked since the last pay, and MannaBooks works out the most they can have.
3. **Finance records the payment** after sending the money by bank, M-Pesa or cash. It goes to **Staff advances** (account 1470), money the employee owes the business, not an expense.

Anyone who can decide can decline it with a note, which the employee sees. The employee can cancel until it's paid. Emails go out when it's asked for, recommended, approved or declined.

## Taking it back

The next payroll adds a **Salary advance** deduction to their payslip by itself:

- monthly staff: on the monthly payroll, split equally over the payslips agreed;
- waged staff: on their next pay, monthly or casual, once their days or hours are filled in.

When the payroll is approved, what was taken back reduces **Staff advances**, and the advance shows what's left. If the payroll is reopened, those amounts are put back.

**A third of their pay is always left.** Kenyan law doesn't let deductions take more than two thirds of someone's pay. If a payslip can't take the full amount back, it takes what it can and the rest comes off the next one.

On someone's **last pay** (their exit date falls in the pay period), whatever is left is taken back in full, as far as the one-third rule allows.

You can change the amount on the payslip line, or remove it to take it back next time.

## The rules

On **People > Salary advances**, finance can open **Rules** to:

- switch advances on or off for the business;
- set the most staff can have, as a percentage;
- set the longest time to pay back, in months.

## Tax

An advance isn't income, so there's no PAYE, SHIF, NSSF or Housing Levy on it. Those are worked out on the full pay as normal, and the advance comes off afterwards.
