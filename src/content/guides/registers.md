# Registers

**Organisation > Registers** keeps what the business has and pays for, by kind: software and subscriptions, licences and permits, insurance policies, vehicles, domains, contracts, or any register you set up. Each register is a list of items with the same core: who supplies it, what it costs and how often, when it renews, who is responsible, who uses it, seats, and its cost centre, plus a few fields of its own.

## Who sees what

- **Everyone** sees every register's items, so anyone can check *do we have Canva?* with the search box, and **ask for access**.
- **Costs** show to roles with **Registers: view**, and to the person responsible for an item.
- **Registers: manage** adds registers and items, changes them and decides who gets access. The person responsible for an item can change it and decide too.

## Starting a register

**New register**, then choose a preset (each comes with its own fields) or start from nothing. Under **Register settings** change its name and description, whether its items have **seats**, and up to eight fields of its own: text, a number, a date, or a choice from a list. A register with items can be switched off (they're kept); an empty one can be deleted.

## Items

**Add** opens the form: name, supplier, who's responsible, cost and how often it's paid, the renewal or expiry date, how many days before to remind, cost centre, the register's own fields, the departments and people who use it, and seats. Keep notes short and **never store passwords**: use a password manager.

- **Renews by itself:** once the date passes, it moves on by its cycle. Untick for something that expires (a permit): it stays overdue until you change it.
- **Ended:** set an item to Ended to keep its record without counting it.
- **Files:** the contract, policy or receipts, on the item's page.

## Renewals

The person responsible (or, with nobody responsible, whoever manages registers) is reminded by email and in the bell before each renewal. Renewals show on **Coming up**, and in the **cash projection** as money out on each date and cycle.

## Asking for access

On any item, **Ask for access** and say what you need it for. The person responsible decides on the item's page (also listed at the top of Registers, and on their **My day**). Giving access adds the person to its users; when every seat is taken, add a seat first.
