# Departments and in-charges

A **department** groups staff (Sales, Stores, Finance, HR). Each department **runs modules**, and its **head** is the **in-charge** of those modules.

## Why in-charges matter

The in-charge of a module:

- receives its approvals (for example, an order above a member's approval limit);
- receives its alerts and escalations;
- will be asked for its reports.

If a module has no in-charge, these go to the Owner.

| Example department | Runs | Head |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stores | Inventory and stores, Purchasing | Store manager |
| Finance | Finance and accounting, Cash and banking | Chief accountant |
| HR | HR and payroll | HR manager |

A module can belong to **one** department only, so there is never doubt about who is in charge.

## Setting one up

1. Add the head as an **employee** first.
2. Choose **New department**, give it a name, pick the **head**, and tick the modules it runs.
3. Put staff in the department from their employee record.

A department with employees can't be deleted; move them first.

## My department (for heads)

A department's head sees **My department** in the menu. It shows, for each department they head:

- **People:** everyone in the department, and who is on leave today.
- **Leave waiting:** requests from their people that they can decide. Approve them on the Leave page.
- **Money:** income, costs and net this month and this financial year so far, against the department's budget if there is an approved one. It counts everything tagged with the department or one of its cost centres.
- **Work reports:** reports from their people that are late, and how many fall due in the next week.

The menu entry appears for someone set as a department's head, and for people whose role has **Budgets: view own** (Self-service has it to start): they see their own department's people, and its money against its budget, without the head's leave and work reports. Take it off a role under **Roles** to hide budgets from that role.


## All departments (owners and finance)

**All departments** shows every department side by side for the year or month chosen at the top:

- what it **spent**, from the books: everything tagged with the department or one of its cost centres;
- its approved **budget** for the same months, how much of it is **used**, and what's **left** (amber past 85%, red when over);
- its **people** now, and how many are on leave today;
- its **wages**: gross pay on approved payslips, by each person's department, and how many people were paid;
- its **tasks** due in the period: how many are done (and how many on time), how many are overdue, and work reports late now. A task counts in its department, or else in the department of the person doing it.

## A department's page

Click a department, in **Organisation > Departments** or in **All departments**, to open its page. Both lists open the same page; **Edit** on it (or on the row) changes the name, head and modules.

- **Head and people:** who heads it, what it runs, and everyone employed in it. A head employed in another department, such as a CEO who also leads a division, is listed as **Head** too.
- **Documents:** every invoice, bill, expense, payroll and journal in the period with a line tagged to the department or one of its cost centres, with the part that belongs to it. Click one to open it.
- **What it spent on:** account by account (transport, fuel, rent…) against the budget.
- **Pay:** person by person.

People see the head and people with the right to see departments. The money (documents, spending and budget) needs the right to see reports and isn't shown to someone limited to one department; pay needs the right to see payroll. Everything follows the year or month chosen at the top.

## People in a department

When you create or edit a department, tick its people under **People in it**. Ticking someone moves them in from any other department; unticking takes them out.
