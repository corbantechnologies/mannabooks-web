# Departments and in-charges

A **department** groups staff (Sales, Stores, Finance, HR). Each department **runs modules**, and its **head** is the **in-charge** of those modules.

## Why in-charges matter

The in-charge of a module:

- receives its approvals (for example, an order above a member's approval limit);
- receives its alerts and escalations;
- will be asked for its reports.

If a module has no in-charge, these go to the Owner.

| Example department | Runs | Head |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stores | Inventory and stores, Purchasing | Store manager |
| Finance | Finance and accounting, Cash and banking | Chief accountant |
| HR | HR and payroll | HR manager |

A module can belong to **one** department only, so there is never doubt about who is in charge.

## Setting one up

1. Add the head as an **employee** first.
2. Choose **New department**, give it a name, pick the **head**, and tick the modules it runs.
3. Put staff in the department from their employee record.

A department with employees can't be deleted; move them first.

## My department (for heads)

A department's head sees **My department** in the menu. It shows, for each department they head:

- **People:** everyone in the department, and who is on leave today.
- **Leave waiting:** requests from their people that they can decide. Approve them on the Leave page.
- **Money:** income, costs and net this month and this financial year so far, against the department's budget if there is an approved one. It counts everything tagged with the department or one of its cost centres.
- **Work reports:** reports from their people that are late, and how many fall due in the next week.

The menu entry appears only for someone set as a department's head.
