# Sharing with customers

Send a customer a private link to an invoice or to their statement. They open it on their phone without signing in.

## Sharing

- From an invoice: **More > Share link with customer**.
- From a customer: **Share statement**: their balance and every unpaid invoice.

Choose how long the link works (7, 30 or 90 days), then **Copy link**, or send it by **WhatsApp** or **SMS**.

## What the customer sees

Your business name and contacts, the invoice lines and totals, the KRA eTIMS number when signed, and what is left to pay. If M-Pesa is connected they can tap **Pay with M-Pesa**, enter their number and approve the prompt; the payment is recorded against the invoice.

Anyone with the link can see it until it expires.
