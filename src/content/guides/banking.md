# Cash and banking

**Cash and banking** shows every account money sits in: bank accounts, M-Pesa tills and paybills, cash on hand and petty cash, with their balance in the books.

## Accounts

Your starting accounts (Cash on hand, Petty cash, Bank account, M-Pesa) are there already. Choose **Add account** for each other bank account or till; a matching account is added to your chart of accounts.

## Everyday money in and out

- **Customer payments** and **cash sales** (Sales) and **supplier payments** (Purchasing) already move money; you don't enter them again here.
- **Spend money** (Expenses): costs paid on the spot, such as fuel, airtime, rent or bank charges.
- **Receive money** (Other income): interest, rent you receive, commission, refunds.
- **Transfers**: money moved between your own accounts, such as banking the day's cash, sweeping M-Pesa to the bank or topping up petty cash. Enter any bank or M-Pesa charge and it goes to *Bank and M-Pesa charges*.

## Keeping the books right

Once a month, **reconcile** each account against its statement (see *Bank reconciliation*).
