# Chart of accounts

The **chart of accounts** lists every account the business records money in. Each account has a **type**, which decides where it appears in reports:

| Type | What it holds | Normal balance | Report |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assets | What the business owns: cash, bank, M-Pesa, stock, money customers owe | Debit | Balance sheet |
| Liabilities | What it owes: suppliers, VAT, PAYE, loans | Credit | Balance sheet |
| Equity | The owner's stake: capital, retained earnings | Credit | Balance sheet |
| Income | Sales and other income | Credit | Profit and loss |
| Expenses | Cost of sales and running costs | Debit | Profit and loss |

## The starting chart

Every workspace starts with a chart suited to a Kenyan SME: cash, bank and M-Pesa; receivables and payables; VAT, withholding tax, PAYE, SHIF, NSSF and the housing levy; sales, cost of sales and the usual expenses.

Accounts marked **System** are the ones MannaBooks posts to automatically (for example, sales go to *Sales* and VAT to *VAT payable*). You can rename them, but not change their type or switch them off.

## Adding and changing accounts

Choose **New account**, give it a code and a name, and pick its type and detail type. Codes are your own; the starting chart uses 1000s for assets, 2000s liabilities, 3000s equity, 4000s income and 5000s to 6000s expenses.

An account that has entries can't be deleted. Untick **Active** to stop using it; its history stays.

## An account's entries

Click an account's name to see every posted entry in it, with an opening balance, a running balance and a closing balance for the dates you choose.
