# Payment links

A payment link takes one payment, for one thing, by **M-Pesa** or **card**, with no invoice first. Share it on WhatsApp, by email, or print its QR code. Whoever opens it pays on a simple page, and MannaBooks records the payment for you.

Use one for a deposit before a job, a fixed fee, event tickets, a church offering or harambee, school trip money, or gift vouchers.

You need M-Pesa (**Organisation > M-Pesa**) or card payments (**Organisation > Card payments**) switched on. The page offers whichever you have.

## Making a link

1. Open **Sales > Payment links** and choose **New link**.
2. Choose **what the money is for**. This decides how it is recorded:

| For | Becomes | VAT |
|---|---|---|
| **A sale** | A cash sale, with a receipt. A product's stock goes down. | Yes, from the product, or the rate you choose |
| **A deposit or advance** | A payment kept for the customer, to apply to their invoice later | Not yet: VAT is on the invoice |
| **A donation or contribution** | Income (Other income, or the account you choose) | No |
| **A gift voucher** | A voucher, emailed to the payer with its code and PDF | Not yet: VAT is charged when it is spent |

3. Give it a **title** the payer will see, for example *Deposit for kitchen cabinets*.
4. Set the **price**:
    - **One of my products** (sales only), at its selling price and VAT.
    - **A fixed amount**, VAT included.
    - **The payer chooses**, with an optional least amount. This suits deposits, donations and vouchers.
5. Tick **Let them choose how many** for tickets or several of the same item, and set the most they can buy.
6. Optionally set a **last day**, or close it **after this many payments** (for example the number of tickets).

## Sharing it

The link's page shows:
- the link to **Copy**, and a **Share on WhatsApp** button;
- its **QR code**, to download and print on a poster, a counter card or a notice board;
- **views**, **payments** and the **total collected**.

## How someone pays

1. They open the link and see your logo, the title and the price.
2. They enter their name, and choose M-Pesa or card.
    - **M-Pesa:** they enter their number, and a prompt comes to their phone.
    - **Card:** Paystack's secure page opens for the card.
3. The page confirms the payment. They get a receipt by email if they gave one, and you get an email too.

M-Pesa only takes whole shillings, so a price with cents is rounded up when paid by M-Pesa.

## What you see

Each payment appears under the link with who paid, how, and what it became: the **cash sale**, the **deposit** (under the customer, matched by email or phone, or a new customer), or the **voucher**.

If the books couldn't take it (for example the month is closed), the payment shows **Paid** with the reason. Record it by hand.

To apply a deposit, open the customer's payment and apply it to their invoice.

## Closing and deleting

**Close link** stops new payments; **Open again** reopens it. A link nobody has paid through can be deleted. Once someone has paid, it is kept for your records.

## One link, one thing

A payment link is deliberately simple: one purpose, and at most one product. To sell several products in one order, with a cart and delivery, use your **online shop** (**Settings > Online shop**).
