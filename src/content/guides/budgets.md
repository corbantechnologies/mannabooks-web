# Budgets

A budget is your plan for the year's income and spending, month by month. Once approved, MannaBooks compares it with the books as the year goes on.

## Making a budget

1. Open **Accounting > Budgets** and choose **New budget**.
2. Choose the **financial year**, then what it is for:
    - **A department:** covers everything tagged with the department or any of its cost centres, the same figures as *Profit and loss by department*. Its head sees it on **My department**.
    - **A cost centre** or **a project:** just that part.
    - **None of them:** the whole business.

    A cost centre without a budget of its own is checked against its department's budget when someone spends.
3. In the plan, **Add an account** for each income and expense line you want to plan.
4. Type the amount for each month, or type a **year total** in the *Year* column to spread it evenly.
5. **Save budget**.

Every budget is planned **month by month**, so each month's figure is the monthly budget, and *Budget vs actual* compares each month as well as the year to date. Weekly budgets aren't offered: a month is the shortest period most books close on.

## A budget for one month, or a one-off

Not every budget is a year's plan. Under **Covers**, choose **One month, or a one-off** and pick the month. Use it for:

- **a single month's plan**, if you budget a month at a time;
- **a one-off**, such as a staff outing, a launch event or a trip.

Only that month shows in the plan, and it's sent for approval on its own. Several one-month budgets can be approved for the same department, and they **add to** the year's plan: the overspending warning and **All departments** count the year plan and the one-offs together. Budget vs actual for a one-month budget compares that month's spending only.

**Copy last year** fills the plan with what each account actually came to in the same month last year, plus a percentage you choose (for example 5% for inflation). Change any figure afterwards.

## Approving

When the plan is ready, choose **Approve** (or **Send for approval** if an approval rule covers budgets). Only one approved year plan can cover a given year and scope; one-month budgets can sit alongside it. To change an approved budget, **Reopen** it.

## Who sees which budgets

- **Finance and admins** see and manage every budget.
- **Someone limited to a department** sees only that department's budgets (and its cost centres'). When they create one, only their department is offered.
- **A department's head** can draft their department's budget, even without budget rights of their own. They open **Budgets** from the menu, plan the months, then choose **Submit for approval** and, if they like, write what it is for. If an approval rule covers budgets it follows that chain; otherwise everyone who can approve budgets (finance, the CEO) gets an email. The head can't approve it themselves.

Heads plan against account names only; they don't see the books' balances. Once approved, the budget shows on their **My department** page as the figure they're measured against.

## Budget vs actual

The **Budget vs actual** tab shows, for each account, the budget and the actual figure from the start of the year to the month you choose, and the difference. Green means better than planned (more income, or less spending); red means worse. The bar shows how much of the year's budget has been used.

Spending is counted from the books: bills, expenses, payroll and journals posted to that account (and, for a cost centre or project budget, tagged with it).

## Closing a budget

When a budget is done with (the money was spent and paid), choose **Actions > Close (spent and paid)**. It stays approved, so **Budget vs actual** and the spending checks still use it, but it can never be reopened or changed. For any change after that, make a new budget.
