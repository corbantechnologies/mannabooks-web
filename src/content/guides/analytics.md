# Analytics

**Analytics**, in the menu just under Overview, shows what needs attention today. The Overview shows the totals for a month or a year; Analytics shows what to act on now.

## What it shows

- **Sales this month**, before VAT: invoices, debit notes and cash sales. The arrow compares this month so far with the same days last month, so the 10th is compared with the 10th.
- **Money in this month:** customer payments received plus cash sales.
- **Customers owe you**, with how much is overdue.
- **You owe suppliers**, with how much is overdue.
- **Cash and bank:** the balance now, and each account.

Below the totals:

- **Money coming in:**
  - what is overdue, what falls due in the next 7 days, and draft invoices not yet issued;
  - the invoices to chase, with how many days late each one is;
  - the customers who owe the most overdue.
- **Money going out:**
  - overdue bills, bills due in the next 7 days and draft bills not posted;
  - payment vouchers waiting for approval;
  - the bills to pay next.
- **Quotations:**
  - open quotes and their value, and those expiring this week;
  - accepted quotes not yet invoiced, ready to **Convert to invoice**;
  - quotes from catalogues waiting for your review.

Click any line to open it.

## Who sees what

Everyone has the page, but each part shows only to people allowed to see it:

- **Sales:** people who can see invoices or cash sales.
- **Quotations:** people who can see quotations.
- **Money coming in:** people who can see invoices.
- **Money going out:** people who can see supplier bills.
- **Cash and bank:** people who can see banking.

So a cashier sees sales, an accountant sees nearly everything, and an owner sees it all. Someone limited to a department sees only that department's figures, and never the business-wide cash.
