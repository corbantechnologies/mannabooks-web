# Analytics

**Analytics**, in the menu just under Overview, shows what needs attention for a stretch of time you choose. The Overview shows the totals for a month or a year; Analytics shows what to act on.

## Choosing the period

At the top right, pick **This month** (the default), **Next month**, **Last month**, or **Choose dates** for any range, such as the next two weeks or a quarter.

- **Overdue is always as of today**, whatever period you pick. So is what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers in total.
- **What falls due** is counted in the part of the period still ahead. For this month, that's from today to the month end; for next month, the whole month. A period that has already ended has nothing still to fall due.
- **Cash and bank** shows the balances today, or on the last day of a period that has ended.

## What it shows

- **Sales** in the period, before VAT: invoices, debit notes and cash sales. For a period still running, the arrow compares it so far with the same number of days of the period before (the 10th against the 10th). For an ended period, it compares the whole period with the one before. A period that hasn't started shows **Not started**, with the period before for reference.
- **Money in:** customer payments received plus cash sales in the period.
- **Customers owe you**, with how much is overdue.
- **You owe suppliers**, with how much is overdue.
- **Cash and bank:** the balances, and each account.

Below the totals:

- **Money coming in:**
  - what is overdue, what falls due by the end of the period, and draft invoices not yet issued;
  - the invoices to chase, with how many days late each one is;
  - the customers who owe the most overdue.
- **Money going out:**
  - overdue bills, bills due by the end of the period and draft bills not posted;
  - payment vouchers waiting for approval;
  - the bills to pay next.
- **Quotations:**
  - open quotes and their value, and those expiring by the end of the period;
  - accepted quotes not yet invoiced, ready to **Convert to invoice**;
  - quotes from catalogues waiting for your review.

Click any line to open it.

## Who sees what

Everyone has the page, but each part shows only to people allowed to see it:

- **Sales:** people who can see invoices or cash sales.
- **Quotations:** people who can see quotations.
- **Money coming in:** people who can see invoices.
- **Money going out:** people who can see supplier bills.
- **Cash and bank:** people who can see banking.

So a cashier sees sales, an accountant sees nearly everything, and an owner sees it all. Someone limited to a department sees only that department's figures, and never the business-wide cash.
