# Payroll

MannaBooks works out Kenyan payroll each month: **PAYE**, **SHIF**, **NSSF** and the **Affordable Housing Levy**, posts it to the books, and gives staff their payslips.

## Setting up

1. On each employee, open **Pay**, tick **Paid through payroll**, and enter the **basic salary**, how they are paid, and their KRA PIN, NSSF and SHIF numbers.
2. Add regular **allowances** (house, transport) and **deductions** (SACCO, welfare, loan). Untick *Taxed* for tax-free allowances such as genuine per diem.
3. Check **Payroll > Statutory rates** against the latest KRA, SHA and NSSF notices. When a rate changes, add a new set from the date it applies.

## Each month

1. **Run payroll** for the month: a payslip is made for everyone on payroll. Approved unpaid leave reduces basic pay.
2. **Edit** a payslip for this month's extras: overtime, bonus, an advance.
3. **Approve**. The payroll is posted to the books: salaries and the employer's NSSF and Housing Levy as costs; PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, Housing Levy, other deductions and net pay as amounts owed. Staff can now see their payslips.
4. Download the **Bank / M-Pesa list** to pay salaries, then **Record salaries paid**.
5. Use the **Statutory schedule** to file returns, and **Record paid** for each one when you pay KRA, SHA and NSSF (by the 9th of the next month).

## How the tax is worked out

NSSF is 6% of pay up to the upper limit, from both employee and employer. SHIF is 2.75% of gross pay (at least KES 300). The Housing Levy is 1.5% from each. PAYE is charged on pay after NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy, in bands from 10% to 35%, less personal relief of KES 2,400. Open a payslip and choose *How the tax was worked out* to see each step.

An approved payroll can be **reopened** to correct it until it is paid.
