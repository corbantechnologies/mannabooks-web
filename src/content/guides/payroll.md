# Payroll

MannaBooks works out Kenyan payroll each month: **PAYE**, **SHIF**, **NSSF** and the **Affordable Housing Levy**, posts it to the books, and gives staff their payslips. It also pays **casuals and waged workers** for any period, with or without those deductions.

## Setting up

1. On each employee, open **Pay** and choose how they are **paid**:
    - **A monthly salary:** enter the basic salary.
    - **A daily or hourly wage:** enter the rate. The days or hours go on each payslip.
2. Tick **On the monthly payroll** for everyone paid every month, including waged staff and monthly casuals. Leave it off for casuals paid now and then.
3. Under **Statutory deductions**, untick what the person doesn't pay. For example, a casual with no NSSF, SHIF or Housing Levy, or someone with none at all. The employer's NSSF and Housing Levy follow the same ticks.
4. Enter where the money goes (bank, M-Pesa or cash), and their KRA PIN, NSSF and SHIF numbers.
5. Add regular **allowances** (house, transport) and **deductions** (SACCO, welfare, loan). Untick *Taxed* for tax-free allowances such as genuine per diem.
6. Check **Payroll > Statutory rates** against the latest KRA, SHA and NSSF notices. When a rate changes, add a new set from the date it applies. Each payslip uses the one set in force on its pay date; older sets stay for history.

## Each month

1. **Pay staff > Monthly payroll** for the month: a payslip is made for everyone on the monthly payroll. Approved unpaid leave reduces basic pay. There is one monthly payroll a month.
2. **Edit** a payslip for this month's extras (overtime, bonus, an advance), the days or hours of a waged worker, or which deductions apply this month. **Add someone** or **Remove** a person while it's a draft.
3. **Approve**. The payroll is posted to the books: salaries and the employer's NSSF and Housing Levy as costs; PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, Housing Levy, other deductions and net pay as amounts owed. Staff can now see their payslips.
4. Download the **Bank / M-Pesa list** to pay salaries, then **Record salaries paid**.
5. Use the **Statutory schedule** to file returns, and **Record paid** for each one when you pay KRA, SHA and NSSF (by the 9th of the next month).

## Casuals and wage pay

**Pay staff > Casual or wage pay** pays anyone, for any period, as often as you need: a week of site casuals, a fortnight, a single job, or a whole month.

1. Give it a title if you like, the dates it covers, and a pay date.
2. Choose who to pay. Anyone active can be paid, on the monthly payroll or not. People on a daily or hourly wage are ticked to start with.
3. **Deduct statutory** is off, so everyone is paid in full. Switch it on to deduct; each person's own ticks still apply.
4. On each payslip, enter the **days or hours** worked, or the **amount** for someone without a wage rate.
5. **Approve**, pay, and record it as for the monthly payroll. It is posted to salaries in the books with its own reference.

A payslip with nothing to pay stops approval, so nobody is approved at zero by mistake.

## How the tax is worked out

NSSF is 6% of pay up to the upper limit, from both employee and employer. SHIF is 2.75% of gross pay (at least KES 300). The Housing Levy is 1.5% from each. PAYE is charged on pay after NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy, in bands from 10% to 35%, less personal relief of KES 2,400. Open a payslip and choose *How the tax was worked out* to see each step. A deduction switched off for someone is simply left out: for example, with NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy off, PAYE is charged on the whole taxable pay.

An approved payroll can be **reopened** to correct it until it is paid.
