# Projects

A **project** is a job or contract whose income and costs you want to see together: building works, a consultancy assignment, an event, an installation.

## Starting a project

Open **Projects** and choose **New project**. Enter the name, the customer, the **contract value** (what the customer pays, before VAT) and the **cost budget** (what the job should cost). The code is numbered for you (PRJ-0001) unless you type one.

## Tagging income and costs

Pick the project on:

- **invoices** and quotations for the work;
- **bills**, purchase and service orders for materials and subcontractors;
- **expenses** paid on the spot (fuel, casual labour, transport).

Stock sold or used on an invoice for the project counts as a project cost too.

## Following the job

The project page shows:

- **Income, costs and profit**, and the margin;
- how much of the **cost budget** is used, with a warning colour as it runs out;
- what has been **invoiced**, what the customer still owes, and what is **left to invoice** on the contract;
- the figures by account, and the latest transactions.

When the job is done, set its status to **Completed**. Cancelled projects are no longer offered on documents.
