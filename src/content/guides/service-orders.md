# Service orders

A **service order** is work you order from a supplier: repairs, cleaning, transport, consultancy.

1. Open **Purchasing > Service orders**, choose **New service order**, describe the work and the agreed price, and choose the expense account for each line (for example *Repairs and maintenance*).
2. It is **approved** by someone with approval rights.
3. When the work is finished, the person who requested it chooses **Confirm work done**, with a rating and notes. This is the **service completion**.
4. When the supplier's invoice arrives, choose **Enter bill** on the order: the lines are copied.

Confirming completion before paying stops money going out for work that wasn't done.
