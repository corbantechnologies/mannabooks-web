# Delivery notes

A **delivery note** goes with the goods. The customer signs it to confirm what arrived. It records quantities only; the money is on the invoice.

## Making one

1. Open the invoice and choose **More > Delivery note**: the lines are copied for you. Or open **Sales > Delivery notes** and choose **New delivery note**.
2. Check the quantities: for a part delivery, reduce them or remove lines.
3. Add the vehicle, driver, contact person and address (blank uses the customer's address).
4. **Print** two copies: one stays with the customer, the other comes back signed.

## Tracking it

- **Mark dispatched** when the vehicle leaves.
- **Mark delivered** when it arrives, with the name of the person who received it.
- **Void** a delivery note made by mistake.

A draft can still be edited; once dispatched it can't.
