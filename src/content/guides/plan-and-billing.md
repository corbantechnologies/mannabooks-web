# Plan and billing

MannaBooks has a free plan and three paid plans. Every new business starts with **30 days of every feature**, then chooses a plan or carries on with **Free**.

| Plan | For | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | One person starting out | Quotations, invoices, cash sales, expenses and M-Pesa matching; 1 team member; 30 documents a month |
| **Biashara** | Service businesses | All of sales and banking, VAT, CRM, projects and payroll; 3 team members |
| **Duka** | Shops, restaurants, stores | Adds the till, stock, buying, recipes and the online shop; 5 team members |
| **Kampuni** | Growing and multi-branch firms | Everything, including budgets, approvals, fixed assets and company groups; 15 team members |

See today's prices on **Organisation > Plan and billing** or the public pricing page. Payroll is an add-on charged per employee on the payroll. **Accountants and self-service staff never use up a seat.**

## Paying

1. Open **Organisation > Plan and billing** (Owners and Admins).
2. Choose a plan, then **monthly** or **yearly** (a year costs ten months).
3. Enter a discount code if you have one, and your M-Pesa number.
4. Approve the prompt on your phone. The plan runs on from the end of the time already paid.

You can also pay from the M-Pesa menu: **Lipa na M-Pesa > Paybill**, the MannaBooks paybill number, and your business's **account number** (it starts with MB and is shown on the page). It is matched to your business automatically.

## Reminders

Owners and Admins get an email 7 days and 1 day before the trial ends or the plan renews, the day after a payment is missed, and when the business turns read-only. Each reminder is sent once.

## If a payment is missed

- The day after the paid time ends, the plan shows **Payment due**.
- After 14 more days the business becomes **read-only**: you can still open, print and export everything, but not change it.
- Paying brings everything back at once. Nothing is ever deleted.

You can move to **Free** at any time. Parts of MannaBooks your plan doesn't include stay readable, so your records are always there.

## Founding businesses

Businesses that joined during the pilot are free until MannaBooks launches, then get a discount on their first year.
