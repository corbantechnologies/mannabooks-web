# Tasks

A task is something to do by a date, and a time if it matters: collect the signed lease, fix the gate, bank the takings by 3pm, renew the business permit. Give one to yourself, to the people below you, or to any colleague if your role allows it. Tasks aren't tied to work reports or to employee records: anyone with a login can be given one.

## Giving a task

Open **Tasks** and choose **New task**:

1. Say what the task is, with any details: where, who to see, what done looks like.
2. **Who does it.** Leave it on yourself for your own to-do, or tick one or more people. Each person gets their own task, so you can see who has done theirs.
3. **Due on** and **By (time)**. Leave the time blank for the end of the day, or leave both blank for no deadline.
4. **Priority.** High-priority tasks are flagged in the list and in the email.
5. **I'll check it when it's done.** On: when they mark it done, it waits for you to accept it. Off: done is done. Your own tasks never wait for a check.

Who you can give tasks to:

- yourself;
- the people below you in the reporting line (from **Reports to** on employee records), and your department if you head it;
- **any colleague**, if your role has the **Tasks: assign** permission. Admins, accountants, HR, sales and store managers have it; you can change this under roles.

## Doing a task

Your tasks are under **My tasks**, with what's due first. Open one, then:

- **I've started**: the person who gave it sees **In progress**.
- **Mark done**, with a note if it helps (what was done, where it is). If they asked to check it, it shows as **Done, to check** until they do.

## Checking a task

Tasks you gave are under **I gave**. When one is marked done, you get an email. Open it, then **Accept** it, or **Send back** with what is still needed. A sent-back task returns to the person as **In progress**.

You can also **Change** a task's details or deadline while it is open, **Cancel** it, or **Reopen** a done or cancelled one.

## Reminders and overdue

- On the morning of the due day, the person gets a reminder.
- MannaBooks checks every hour. Once a task passes its due time, it shows as **overdue** in red, and the person who gave it gets one email.
- A task marked done after its time is marked **done late**, even if it is later sent back and done again.

## Seeing everyone's tasks

People whose role has the **Tasks: view** permission (admins and HR) get an **Everyone's** tab.

## Tasks and work reports

Tasks are for one-off jobs. For something that repeats every day, week or month, or goes to a whole team, use a **repeating task** under **Work reports** (see [Work reports](work-reports)). Those count in each person's on-time record.
