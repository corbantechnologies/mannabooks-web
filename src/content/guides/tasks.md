# Tasks

A task is something to do by a date, and a time if it matters: collect the signed lease, fix the gate, bank the takings by 3pm, renew the business permit. Give one to yourself, to the people below you, to any colleague if your role allows it, or to a whole department for anyone in it to take. Tasks aren't tied to work reports or to employee records: anyone with a login can be given one.

## Giving a task

Open **Tasks** and choose **New task**:

1. Say what the task is, with any details: where, who to see, what done looks like. Add a **checklist** (one step per line) if it has several steps, and link it to a **project** or **customer** if it belongs to one.
2. **Who does it.** Leave it on yourself for your own to-do, or tick one or more people, or a department. Each person (and each department) gets their own task, so you can see who has done theirs. If you tick someone who is also in a department you ticked, MannaBooks asks whether you mean the department, the person, or both, so the same job isn't given twice by mistake.
3. **Due on** and **By (time)**. Leave the time blank for the end of the day, or leave both blank for no deadline.
4. **Priority.** High-priority tasks are flagged in the list and in the email.
5. **I'll check it when it's done.** On: when they mark it done, it waits for you to accept it. Off: done is done. Your own tasks never wait for a check.

Who you can give tasks to:

- yourself;
- the people below you in the reporting line (from **Reports to** on employee records), and your department if you head it;
- **any colleague**, if your role has the **Tasks: assign** permission. Admins, accountants, HR, sales and store managers have it; you can change this under roles.

## Department tasks

When you don't mind who does it, give the task to a department: under **Who does it**, tick the department (for example **Sales department**) instead of a person.

- **Who can:** anyone with **Tasks: assign** can give a task to any department; a department head can give one to their own department.
- **Who hears about it:** for a **high-priority** task, everyone in the department gets an email. Otherwise only the head does, so people aren't flooded. Everyone in the department sees it either way, under **My tasks > For your department, not taken yet**.
- **Taking it:** anyone in the department chooses **I'll take it**. It becomes theirs and leaves the others' lists. If you take a department task you gave yourself, it doesn't wait for anyone to check it.
- **Handing it out:** the head can give it to a named person instead, from the task page, or move it from one person to another.
- **Handing it back:** if the person can't finish it, **Hand back to the department** returns it for someone else to take. The head is told.
- **Who held it:** the task page lists everyone who took it, handed it back or was given it, with the times.

You see it under **I gave** as *Sales department, nobody has taken it yet*, then with the person's name once someone has it.

If nobody takes it, the head gets the due-day reminder. If it passes its due time untaken, you and the head are told it's overdue and nobody has it.

### Performance

- **Each person:** whoever marks it done gets the credit, on time or late. Their profile shows a **Tasks done on time** figure, with how many they took from their department and how many they handed back.
- **Each department:** the **Departments** tab (for heads, and for roles with **Tasks: view**) shows, per department, how many tasks it was given, how many are waiting, how long they usually wait before someone takes them, how many were done on time or late, and how many were handed back.

## Doing a task

Your tasks are under **My tasks**, with what's due first. Open one, then:

- **I've started**: the person who gave it sees **In progress**.
- **Mark done**, with a note if it helps (what was done, where it is). If they asked to check it, it shows as **Done, to check** until they do.

## Working on a task

Open a task to see everything about it:

- **Checklist:** the steps. Whoever does the task (or gave it) ticks them off; the bar shows how far along it is.
- **Files:** the brief, drafts, the finished document. PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or photos, up to 10 MB each.
- **Comments:** questions and updates. Whoever gave the task and whoever does it are emailed about each new comment or file.
- **Project and customer**, if it's linked to one.

## List or board

Choose **List** or **Board** above the tasks. The board shows the same tasks in columns: **To do**, **In progress**, **Done, to check** and **Done**. Drag a card to another column to move it, where you're allowed to (start it, mark it done, accept it). Each card shows its due date, priority, checklist progress, comments and files. MannaBooks remembers your choice.

In the list, open tasks come first, overdue and soonest due at the top, then higher priority. Tick **Show done and cancelled** to see finished ones in a **Finished** section underneath, latest first.

To see only one kind, choose it in **Status**: Not started, In progress, Done to check, Done or Cancelled. Choosing **Done** lists every done task, without scrolling past the open ones.

**Group by status** puts the list under headings in the order work moves: Done to check, In progress, To do, Done and Cancelled, finished tasks included. Each heading folds, so you can close the ones you don't need.

## Insights

The **Insights** tab, and a **Tasks** section on the **Overview**, show the numbers:

- how many tasks there are, and what share are **done**, **in progress**, **not started** and **late** (done after their time, or still open past it);
- a bar for each person showing how much they carry, done, in progress and not started, so you can see who is overloaded and who has room;
- tables by department and by person, with how many are done on time.

Choose whose numbers to see, and the period:

- **Due this week** (Monday to Sunday), **Due this month** or **Due this year**: the tasks with a deadline in that period, whenever they were given;
- **Last 30 or 90 days**, **Last year** or **All time**: the tasks given in that time.

Who can see which numbers:

- **Whole company** and **By department**: owners, admins and HR (roles with **Tasks: view**);
- **My departments**: department heads, for their departments and their people;
- **My team**: anyone people report to, for everyone below them;
- **Just me**: everyone, for their own tasks.

## Changing the status from the list

Each task in the list has its status as a small dropdown, so you don't have to open it. The person doing it can move it to **In progress** or **Done**; the person who gave it can **Accept** it, **Cancel** it or **Reopen** it (for your own tasks, that's you: cancel a duplicate or something no longer needed, without counting it as done). **Send back** opens the task, because it needs a reason.

## Checking a task

When someone finishes a task you gave and asked to check, you get an email, and the task waits under **Tasks > To check** (the tab shows how many). It also shows at the top of **Approvals**. Open it, then **Accept** it, or **Send back** with what is still needed. A sent-back task returns to the person as **In progress**.

A task you didn't ask to check is simply done. It stays under **I gave** for a week after it is finished, so the "Done" email always has a task to open. After that, choose **Status: Done** to find it.

You can also **Change** a task's details or deadline while it is open, **Cancel** it, or **Reopen** a done or cancelled one.

## Reminders and overdue

- On the morning of the due day, the person gets a reminder.
- MannaBooks checks every hour. Once a task passes its due time, it shows as **overdue** in red, and the person who gave it gets one email.
- Times follow the business's **time zone** in **Settings** (Nairobi unless changed).
- A task marked done after its time is marked **done late**, even if it is later sent back and done again.

## Seeing everyone's tasks

People whose role has the **Tasks: view** permission (admins and HR) get an **Everyone's** tab.

## Tasks and work reports

Tasks are for one-off jobs. For something that repeats every day, week or month, or goes to a whole team, use a **repeating task** under **Work reports** (see [Work reports](work-reports)). Those count in each person's on-time record.

## Finding tasks

Search by title, people, department, project or customer, and filter by **who**, **priority** and **when due**. **Group by** priority, due date, week, department or person; each group folds away with its arrow. Your choices are remembered.

## Who can change a task

Only whoever gave a task can change it, check it or cancel it. A tasks manager can too, except on a task given to them or to their own department. Nobody changes a deadline they were given or checks their own work. Tasks waiting for your check also show at the top of **Approvals**, with a count on the menu.

## Several tasks at once (a chain)

**Several tasks** gives a whole list in one go, such as onboarding, a job's stages or month-end. For each task you can set:

- **Opens on**: locked until that date. People see it, but can't start it.
- **Waits until the one above is done**: locked until the task above it is done, and checked where it needs checking.

Everyone chosen gets their own copy of the list. When a task opens, the person is told. A single task can also have an **Opens on** date.

## People without a login yet

A new hire you've added as an employee, but not yet invited, shows under **No login yet** when you give tasks. The tasks wait for them, marked *no login yet*, and become theirs the moment they accept their invitation.

## Messages

Everything MannaBooks emails you about the business (a task given, a report due, an approval waiting) also lands under the **bell** at the top, with the unread count, and on **Work > Messages**.
