# Bank reconciliation

Reconciling proves your books agree with the bank or M-Pesa statement. Do it every month for every account.

## 1. Import the statement

Open the account in **Cash and banking** and choose **Import statement**. Download the statement as CSV from your bank's internet banking or the M-Pesa statement (open the PDF in Excel and save as CSV if needed). MannaBooks guesses the columns (date, details, money in, money out); check them and import. Lines already imported are skipped.

## 2. Match each line

For each line **To match**, choose **Match**:

- MannaBooks shows transactions in the books with the same amount within 10 days. Pick the right one.
- If it isn't in the books (bank charges, interest, a standing order), choose the account and **Record and match**. It is entered as an expense or other income.
- Lines that don't belong (for example a reversal pair) can be **ignored**.

## 3. Reconcile

Choose **Reconcile**, and enter the statement date and **closing balance**. Matched transactions are ticked for you; tick any others that appear on the statement. When the **Difference** is 0.00, choose **Finish**.

Transactions in a finished reconciliation are locked: a document behind one can't be voided or changed until you **Reopen** the reconciliation (only the latest can be reopened). Unticked items, such as cheques not yet cleared, carry forward to next month.
