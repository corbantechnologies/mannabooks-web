# Stock takes

A **stock take** is a physical count. Do one at least every month end, or for one category at a time.

1. **Inventory > Stock takes > Start a count**: choose the warehouse and, if you like, one category.
2. MannaBooks lists each product with what the books say. **Print count sheet** for the people counting.
3. Enter what was counted. **Save counts** as you go; leave a product blank if it wasn't counted.
4. **Post**: each counted product moves to the counted quantity. Shortages and extras are valued at average cost and posted to *Stock adjustments and losses*.

Count when the shop is closed, or post quickly: the difference is worked out against the books when the count started.
