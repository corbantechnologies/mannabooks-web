# My day

**My day** is a checklist at the top of your **Overview**. It shows what to get through each day, and it starts fresh every morning.

## The lines

Some lines count what is waiting for you and tick themselves when nothing is:

- **Review what's waiting for your approval:** orders, vouchers, payroll and budgets at your level.
- **Check tasks people have finished:** tasks you gave that are done and waiting for your check.
- **Hand in your work reports and repeating tasks due:** due today or overdue.
- **Write or give goal reviews:** your own goals that have ended, and your people's waiting for your review.
- **Go through the Business inbox:** new documents sent to you by other businesses (if you can see the inbox).

A number shows how many are waiting. Click the line to go there. Once they are dealt with, the line ticks itself. You can also tick it by hand, for example when the rest can wait until tomorrow.

Other lines are ticked by hand, such as **Check the cash, bank and M-Pesa balances**. Opening the line's page ticks it too.

## Take your tasks for the day

The line **Take your tasks for the day** is ticked by picking tasks:

1. Click **Pick from your tasks**.
2. Tick the open tasks you mean to work on today.
3. Put them in order with the arrows.
4. Click **Save today's tasks**.

They show under the line, so you can open each one from the Overview. Change them whenever you like. Tomorrow you pick again.

## Your own list

Click **Edit my list** to:

- **add lines of your own**, such as "Call two customers", which only you see;
- **take a business line off your own list** by unticking it. It stays on everyone else's.

## The business's checklist

Owners and admins (Workspace settings: manage) choose the lines everyone starts with under **Settings > Daily checklist**, or **Business checklist** on the card. For each line you can:

- **add one**, such as "Open the till and count the float", with a page it opens (for example /pos);
- **choose who it is for:** everyone, a role, a department or one person;
- **switch it off** or move it up and down.

The built-in lines (approvals, tasks to check, reports, goal reviews, inbox, balances, today's tasks) can be switched off or given to some people, but not deleted.

To take the card off your Overview, or move it, use **Customise** on the Overview.
