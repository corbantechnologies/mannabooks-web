# Online shop

Your own shop page on the web, where customers order the products you choose and pay by M-Pesa or when they collect. Orders go straight into MannaBooks; there is nothing to retype.

## Opening the shop

1. **Settings > Online shop**: tick **Shop is open**, write a short welcome, and choose:
   - whether customers can **collect**, you **deliver**, or both, and the **delivery fee**;
   - whether they may **pay on pickup or delivery**.
2. On each product to sell online, open it in **Sales > Products**, tick **Sell online**, and add a picture link if you have one.
3. Copy your shop link from Settings (it looks like `…/shop/your-business`) and share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or your website.

To let customers pay by M-Pesa when they order, connect your paybill or till in **M-Pesa (Daraja)**. Without it, customers pay when they collect or receive their order.

## What customers see

- Your products with their prices **including VAT**, by category, with a search. Stock items that have run out show *Out of stock*.
- A basket, then their name, phone, collect or deliver (and where), and how they pay.
- Paying by M-Pesa sends a prompt to their phone straight away; they enter their PIN and the order shows *Paid*.
- A private **order page** that shows where the order is: *Received*, *Confirmed*, *Ready*, *Completed*. If a prompt failed, they can send it again from there.

## Handling orders

New orders appear in **Sales > Online orders**, with a count in the menu.

1. **Confirm** it once you have checked you can fill it; the customer sees *Confirmed*.
2. **Mark ready** when it is packed: *Come and collect it* or *It's on its way*.
3. When the customer has it, choose **Collected** or **Delivered: complete**.
   - Paid by M-Pesa online: the payment is used for the sale.
   - Paying now: choose how they paid (cash, M-Pesa code, card or bank).

Completing records a **cash sale** at the products' prices, with delivery as its own line, and takes the items out of stock. Find it in **Sales > Cash sales**.

**Cancel** an order you can't fill, with a reason the customer sees. An order already paid by M-Pesa must be refunded first.

## Good to know

- Stock is checked when the order is placed and again when you complete it; the shop never sells what you have marked out of stock.
- M-Pesa takes whole shillings, so a total with cents is rounded up on the prompt.
- The Cashier and Sales roles can handle online orders; completing one also needs the right to record cash sales.
