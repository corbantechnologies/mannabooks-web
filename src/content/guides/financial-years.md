# Financial years and periods

Your books are divided into **financial years**, each with 12 monthly **periods**. The first year is created with your workspace, starting in the month set in **Settings** (January unless you changed it).

## Closing a month

When a month's books are final (for example, after the VAT return is filed), **Close** it. Nothing can then be posted to, or removed from, that month, by anyone or by any module. If something must change, **Reopen** it; both actions are in the audit log.

## Closing a year

**Close year** does the year-end for you:

1. It posts a closing entry, dated the last day of the year, that moves every income and expense balance into **Retained earnings**.
2. It closes all 12 months.

Close years in order: an earlier open year must be closed first. **Reopen** the most recent closed year to remove its closing entry and reopen its last month.

## Adding years

- **Add next year** creates the year after the latest one. Do this before the new year starts.
- **Add earlier year** creates the year before the earliest one, for example to record opening balances dated before your first year.
