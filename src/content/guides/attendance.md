# Attendance

Attendance records when people start and finish work, from their own phone or computer, and works out each day against their hours. Open it from **People > Attendance**. Attendance comes with every paid plan that has payroll, at no extra cost until the end of 2026. From 2027 it becomes part of the paid People add-on, and you'll be told the price well before then.

## Clocking in and out

On **Attendance** (or **My leave and pay**), click **Clock in** when you start and **Clock out** when you finish. The time is MannaBooks' own clock, in the business's time zone, so a phone's clock can't change it.

- **Breaks:** click **Start break** and **End break**. On a day you don't, the business's fixed break is taken off instead. Clocking out during a break ends the break first.
- **Clocking in again** the same day (for example after going out) is fine: the hours add up.
- **Forgot to clock out?** Some hours after the end time, the day closes itself at the end time and shows **No clock-out**. Ask for a fix if the time is wrong.
- **At the office or away:** if the business has listed its office networks, each clock-in shows whether it was from the office Wi-Fi. Phones on mobile data count as away.

Your login must be linked to your employee record. If Attendance says it isn't, ask HR.

You can also clock in, take a break and clock out from the dot on your initial at the top right, on any page.

## Asking for a fix

On **My days**, click **Ask for a fix**: the day, what to fix (clock-in, clock-out or a break), the right time and why. Your supervisor (the person you report to) or your department head approves or declines. The original time stays on record, marked as replaced.

## Who's in now

Supervisors, department heads and HR see **Who's in now**: everyone on their team with their status (*In*, *On a break*, *Not in yet*, *Gone home*, *On leave*, *Off today*), their times and hours, and whether they are **active now** in MannaBooks (or when they were last seen). Nothing else is watched: no screens, no location during the day.

## The day register

For people who don't clock in themselves, such as casuals without a phone, supervisors keep the **Day register**: pick the day and mark each person **Present**, **Half day** or **Absent**, then **Save the register**. A mark doesn't replace someone's own clock-in.

## Corrections to decide

**Corrections** lists the fixes your team has asked for. **Approve** adds the corrected time; **Decline** asks for a reason, which the person sees. You get a message in the bell when one arrives.

## Shifts

**Shifts** (on Attendance, for HR, department heads and supervisors) shows the **rota**: who is on which shift, day by day, two weeks at a time.

- **New shift:** a name, and either **fixed times** or **flexible hours a week**. A shift that ends before it starts, such as *Night, 22:00 to 06:00*, runs into the next morning and counts as the day it started. Tick the days it covers (none ticked: each person's usual working days), and give it its own grace or break if it needs one. A shift can be for the whole business or one department.
- **Put people on a shift** for a date range, such as the night rota for two weeks or a special project for a month. While someone is on a shift, its hours, days, grace and break replace their usual ones: lateness, absences, alerts, Who's in now, Months and pay all follow it. Afterwards they go back to their usual hours by themselves.
- A person is on one shift at a time. Someone already on a shift in the chosen dates is named, so you can end that one first (**End today**) or choose other dates.
- **Who sets shifts:** HR (*Attendance: manage*) for anyone. A department head for their own department, and puts their own people on its shifts.
- A shift people have been on is kept for the record. Untick **In use** to switch it off.

## Months and pay

**Months** shows each person's month: days worked (and half days), absences, leave, days late, hours, extra hours, and what a waged payslip pays. Everyone sees their own (**My months**).

When a month has ended, the person's supervisor or department head clicks **Approve**. They can change the extra hours before approving. The month is then **locked**: no more corrections or register changes. HR can **Open again** until the month's payroll is approved.

What approved months feed:

- **Monthly payroll:** daily-waged staff are paid the approved days (a register mark of *Half day*, or a day shorter than a full day, pays a half), hourly-waged staff the approved hours. Approved extra hours become an **Extra hours** line on the payslip, at the business's rates.
- **Casual pay:** the days or hours each casual worked in the period fill in by themselves.
- **Salary advances:** for waged staff, the days or hours worked since they were last paid come from attendance.
- **Salaried staff:** absences are listed for HR. Pay is never cut automatically.

## Extra hours

Extra hours are counted **by the week**: time worked on working days beyond the weekly threshold (45 hours to start), plus every hour worked on a rest day or public holiday. A week that crosses into another month is shared out by its working days. HR sets the threshold and the rates (1.5 times on normal days and 2 times on rest days and holidays to start), and whether extra hours need approval.

## Alerts

Each alert can be switched on or off and timed by HR. They arrive by email and in the bell, once:

| Alert | Who is told |
| --- | --- |
| Not clocked in after the start time and grace | The person, then their supervisor |
| Still clocked in after the end time | The person |
| Flexible hours short for the week (Friday afternoon to start) | The person |
| A correction to decide | The supervisor or department head |
| A task due today given to someone who isn't in | Whoever gave the task |

## The break rule

HR can switch on a **break rule** under Attendance **Settings**: a break of at least so many minutes after so many hours worked in one go (for example 30 minutes after 6 hours). Every part is the business's to set:

- **Remind them before** it's due (15 minutes to start; 0 for no reminder).
- A **missed break** is flagged on the person's day and in **Hours vs pay**; they're told, and their supervisor too if ticked.
- **Paid or unpaid**: a paid break counts as work. For unpaid breaks, **take a missed break off the hours anyway** if the rule is that the break is unpaid whether taken or not.
- Leave a department or person out under **Departments and people with their own hours** (Break rule: doesn't apply).

It needs the **Start break** and **End break** buttons on: they're how MannaBooks knows a break was taken. A pause shorter than the rule's break doesn't count as one.

## Hours vs pay

**Attendance > Hours vs pay** sets each person's hours this month against the hours expected so far and, for those who can see payroll (and each person for themself), against their pay: an hour is worth the monthly salary over the monthly hours (160 to start). Under is below 90% of the hours expected, over is above 110%. It also counts weeks below the floor, weeks near overtime and missed breaks.

Switch on **Hours against pay** in Settings to also send, every Monday, a word about last week to the person (and their supervisor, if ticked): under the **weekly floor** (10 hours to start), or **near overtime** (40 hours to start) so the work can be shared out. Nobody's pay changes here: extra hours are still paid through **Months**, once approved.

## Giving out tasks

When Attendance is on, the task form shows whether each person is in today, whether they're active now, and how many open tasks they hold. People who are in come first, with the lightest load at the top.

## Settings (HR and admins)

Everything is set by the business, under **Attendance > Settings** (needs *Attendance: manage*). Every change goes in the audit log.

- **Switch Attendance on or off.**
- **Working hours:** fixed (a start and end time) or flexible (hours a week, with optional core hours when everyone must be in). Working days follow the business's working week, or a person's own week on their employee record. Public holidays and approved leave are days off.
- **Grace before late:** clocking in within this many minutes of the start isn't late (15 to start with).
- **Breaks:** the fixed unpaid break, and whether the break buttons show.
- **Forgotten clock-outs:** how many hours after the end time a day closes itself.
- **Who is tracked:** by worker type (permanent, contract, casual, volunteer, intern; work for hire is off to start, as it is paid per job). Daily-waged staff: what counts as a full day and a half day.
- **Away from the office:** allow it (marked away) or refuse it.
- **Extra hours** and **Alerts**, as above.
- **Office networks:** the office's internet addresses. Open the page on the office Wi-Fi and click **Add this network**, or type an address or range. If your internet provider changes the office's address, add the new one.
- **Departments and people with their own hours:** a department or one person can have their own pattern, hours, grace or break, and a person can be tracked or not whatever their type. Blank boxes follow the level above.
