# Employees and self-service

**Employees** holds the HR record for everyone who works for you, whether or not they sign in to MannaBooks.

## Adding an employee

Choose **New employee**. Their **staff number** is given automatically (EMP-0001, EMP-0002, ...). Fill in what you know; you can complete the rest later.

- **Department** and **Branch**: where they work.
- **Reports to**: their supervisor. Approvals and work reports follow this reporting line, so set it for everyone. MannaBooks stops you making a loop (A reports to B who reports to A).
- **Status**: Active, On leave or Exited.

## Employee self-service

Staff can sign in to see their own record, their payslips and their leave. There are three ways to give someone a login, and each links it to their staff record:

- **When adding them:** tick **Invite them to sign in** on the new employee form (it needs their email) and choose their role.
- **From their record:** open it and choose **Invite to sign in**.
- **From Team and access:** choose **Invite someone**, then pick them under **An employee already on record**. Their email fills in.

They get an email; when they accept, their login is linked to their record. **Self-service** is the usual role for staff who only need their own leave, payslips and details.

Self-service staff see **only their own record**. Under **My details** on their own page they can update it:

- **Phone, email and emergency contact:** saved at once.
- **ID numbers** (national ID, KRA PIN, NSSF, SHIF) **and where their pay goes** (bank or M-Pesa): sent to HR to check first.

A change to where pay goes also emails the person at the address already on file, so a change they didn't make is noticed.

HR sees **Changes to check** beside the person in the staff list. Open their record to **Approve** or **Reject**. A change to where pay goes needs someone who runs payroll, and nobody decides on their own changes.

ID numbers show in full only to payroll staff and the person themself; others see the last three characters (••••678).

## Who can do this

- **Employees: view** sees everyone; **Employees: manage** adds and edits.
- **Employees: view own** (Self-service) sees only themselves.
