# Employees and self-service

**Employees** holds the HR record for everyone who works for you, whether or not they sign in to MannaBooks.

## Adding an employee

Choose **New employee**. Their **staff number** is given automatically (EMP-0001, EMP-0002, ...). Fill in what you know; you can complete the rest later.

- **Department** and **Branch**: where they work.
- **Reports to**: their supervisor. Approvals and work reports follow this reporting line, so set it for everyone. MannaBooks stops you making a loop (A reports to B who reports to A).
- **Status**: Active, On leave or Exited.

## Employee self-service

Staff can sign in to see their own record, and later their payslips, P9 and leave. To give someone a login:

1. Make sure their record has an **email address**.
2. Open their record and choose **Invite to sign in**.
3. They get an email; when they accept, their login is linked to their record with the **Self-service** role.

Self-service staff see **only their own record** and can't change it; they ask HR to correct details.

## Who can do this

- **Employees: view** sees everyone; **Employees: manage** adds and edits.
- **Employees: view own** (Self-service) sees only themselves.
