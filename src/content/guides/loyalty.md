# Loyalty

Reward customers who come back. They earn **points** on what they spend and use them as a discount at the till.

## Setting it up

Open **Sales > Loyalty > Settings and tiers**:

- **Spent for 1 point**: e.g. KES 100 before VAT earns 1 point.
- **1 point is worth**: e.g. KES 1 off a later sale.
- **Least points to redeem** and the most of a sale points can pay for.
- **Points expire after** a number of months (0: never). The oldest points are used first.
- **Tiers** such as Silver and Gold, reached by points earned in the last 12 months, earn faster (e.g. 1.5x).

Switch the programme on and save.

## Members

Enrol a customer under **Sales > Loyalty**. They get a card number (LC-000001), or you can scan a printed card. At the till, find them by phone or card number.

## Earning and redeeming

- Points are earned on cash sales, and on invoices if you allow it, on the amount before VAT.
- At the till, enter the points to use; they come off the sale as a discount, shown on the receipt and sent to KRA eTIMS as a line discount.
- Voiding a sale returns the points it used and takes back the points it earned.

Managers can **adjust points** on a member (a welcome bonus, a correction) with a reason. Every movement is listed on the member's page.
