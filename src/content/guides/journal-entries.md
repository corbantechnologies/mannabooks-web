# Journal entries

Every change to the books is a **journal entry**: a set of lines where the **debits equal the credits**. Most entries are made for you by sales, purchases and payroll. Use a **manual entry** for anything else, for example depreciation, a loan received or correcting a mistake.

## Writing an entry

1. Choose **New entry**, set the date and a description.
2. Add a line for each account, with a debit or a credit (not both).
3. The totals show at the bottom. **Save draft** keeps it to finish later, even if it doesn't balance yet.
4. **Post** it when debits equal credits. Posting gives it a number such as **JE-2026-0001** and locks it.

Example: paying KES 25,000 rent from the bank.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6010 Rent | 25,000.00 | |
| 1100 Bank account | | 25,000.00 |

## Rules MannaBooks checks when posting

- At least two lines, each with a debit or a credit.
- Total debits equal total credits.
- Every account is active.
- The date falls in an **open month** (see Financial years).

## Fixing a posted entry

Posted entries can't be edited or deleted, so the history stays true. Instead, **Reverse** it: MannaBooks posts the same lines with debits and credits swapped on the date you choose, then you post a correct entry. Entries made by other modules are corrected in those modules, not here.

## Who can do what

*Journal entries: create* writes and edits drafts, *post* posts them, *reverse* reverses them. The Accountant role has all three.
