# Audit log

The **audit log** records every change in the workspace: what was created, updated or deleted, by whom, from which address, and when. Nobody can edit or delete it, not even the Owner.

## Reading it

- **Change** shows created, updated or deleted.
- **Record** shows what kind of record changed and its name.
- **Details** shows, for updates, each field's old and new value.

Filter by kind of change, or search for a record by name.

Who can see it: anyone with **Audit log: view** (Owner, Admin and Accountant by default).
