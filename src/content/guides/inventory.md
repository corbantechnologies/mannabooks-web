# Inventory

MannaBooks keeps stock for products with **Track stock** ticked: how many are in each warehouse and what they cost on average.

## Getting started

1. On each product you keep in stock, tick **Track stock** and set **Warn when stock falls to**.
2. **Inventory > Stock > Enter opening stock**: what you have now and what it cost you. It is posted against *Opening balance equity*.
3. Add more **warehouses** if you keep stock in more than one place. Link a warehouse to a branch so that branch's sales take stock from it.

## How stock moves

- **In**: goods received, bills for stock bought without a delivery, customer returns on credit notes, transfers in, production.
- **Out**: invoices, cash sales and the till, transfers out, components used in production.
- MannaBooks won't let stock go below zero. Receive or adjust it first.

Each sale records its **cost of goods sold** at the average cost, so your profit and loss shows real margins.

## Stock screens

- **Stock**: on hand by warehouse, value, and *Low* when at or below the warning level. Export to Excel.
- Click a product for its **stock card**: every movement with its document.
- **Transfers**: move stock between warehouses.
- **Adjustments**: damaged, expired, lost or found stock. The value goes to *Stock adjustments and losses*.
