# Fixed assets

A fixed asset is something the business owns and uses for more than a year: a lorry, laptops, shop fittings, a generator, a building. Its cost is spread over its useful life as **depreciation**, a monthly expense.

## Adding an asset

Open **Accounting > Fixed assets** and choose **Add asset**. Pick the **category**: MannaBooks fills in the usual method, life and account (for example computers over 3 years; vehicles at 25% a year on the reducing balance). Change them if your accountant uses different ones.

How did you get it?

- **Bought with a bill or expense in MannaBooks.** Enter the bill or expense with the line posted to the fixed asset account (such as *Motor vehicles*), then add the asset here. Adding it does not post anything again.
- **Owned before you started using MannaBooks.** Its cost, and the depreciation already charged up to a date, are brought into the books against *Opening balance equity*.

**In use from** is when depreciation starts (the whole month counts). **Worth at the end** (the residual value) is what you expect to sell it for when it is done; it is often 0.

## Methods

- **Straight line:** (cost less residual value) divided by the months of its life, the same every month.
- **Reducing balance:** a yearly percentage of the book value that is left, so the charge falls each year. Common for vehicles and machinery.
- **Not depreciated:** for land.

These are book depreciation. KRA's capital allowances (wear and tear) for your tax return are worked out separately by your tax adviser.

## Running depreciation

At the end of each month open **Fixed assets > Depreciation**, choose the month and **Run depreciation**. The preview shows each asset's charge. One journal is posted: *Depreciation* expense (by cost centre, if the asset has one) against *Accumulated depreciation*. Months that were missed are caught up in the same run.

Made a mistake? **Undo** the latest run, fix the asset, and run it again.

## Selling or scrapping

Run depreciation up to the month before, then open the asset and choose **Sell or scrap**. Enter the date, the money received (0 if scrapped) and the account it went into. MannaBooks removes the asset's cost and depreciation from the books and posts the **gain or loss** (money received less book value).

The **Register** button downloads the full asset register for your auditor.
